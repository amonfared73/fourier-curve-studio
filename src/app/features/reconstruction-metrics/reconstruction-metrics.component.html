These are parameter-space errors: each sample z(tj) is compared with the reconstruction at the same tj = 2πj/M, not with the closest point of the other curve, so they can overstate the geometric distance between the shapes.

A larger N usually improves the reconstruction when the added components carry useful energy. RMSE and MSE at the samples never increase as N grows (each added term removes its |ck|² from the MSE), but the maximum error and the visual/geometric quality need not improve monotonically — especially in largest-magnitude mode, where ringing near corners can shift as terms are added.
